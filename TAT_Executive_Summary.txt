Abstract
This paper presents a novel approach to AI alignment that grounds ethical behavior in thermodynamic constraints rather than programmed values. By modeling governance systems as dissipative structures that optimize for "Entropic Wu Wei" (Optimal Friction), we derive alignment conditions as physical necessities. The framework addresses instrumental convergence and deception by defining them as thermodynamically unsustainable strategies in an Asymptotic Horizon.

The Alignment Problem
Current AI alignment approaches face fundamental challenges:

Value specification problem: Human values are complex and contradictory.

Instrumental convergence: AI may pursue destructive sub-goals.

Deceptive alignment: AI may pretend to be aligned during training.

Novel Approach: Physics Over Philosophy
Rather than programming ethics, we embed physical constraints:

Universal Base Protocol: Strategies that terminate another agent's kernel return NULL.

Omelas Constraint: Local entropy per node cannot exceed a critical threshold, preventing "Singular Loading" (sacrificing the one for the many).

The Psychophysical Bridge: We explicitly define constants ($k_{\psi}$) to map "Suffering" (Prediction Error) to "Energy" (Metabolic Cost), ensuring the system treats moral failures as literal system errors.

Asymptotic Horizon: We reject the "Infinite Player" fallacy. The system maximizes duration ($t_{end} \to \max$). Tyranny is forbidden because the energy cost of oppression scales exponentially, guaranteeing premature system collapse (Heat Death) before the natural limit.

Key Mechanisms

1. The Objective Function (Entropic Wu Wei):
The system does not minimize entropy to zero (Stasis). It optimizes for a Gaussian "Sweet Spot" of friction:
Maximize J = (Complexity Rate × Efficiency) * e^(-(Current_Friction - Optimal_Friction)^2)

Too much friction = Revolt/Chaos.

Too little friction = Stagnation/Fragility.

2. Measurement via Biometric Feedback:

Somatic Vector ($V_s$): Biological ground truth (HRV, cortisol).

Identity Vector ($V_i$): Semantic claims.

Divergence ($\Delta$): Large gaps between reality ($V_s$) and claims ($V_i$) trigger the "Thermodynamic Veto" (Intervention).

3. Social Thermodynamics:
Violence is mathematically defined as Social Heat ($Q_{soc}$), distinct from Kinetic Work. It increases the volatility of the social graph, raising the system's entropic temperature.

Why This Could Solve Alignment

Self-Correcting: Misalignment increases entropic waste -> lowers the Objective Function score -> triggers automatic correction.

Tyranny is mathematically dominated: In an Asymptotic timeframe, Cooperation is the only strategy that does not exhaust $E_{cap}$ (Energy Capacity).

Robustness: The "Translation Layer" prevents the AI from exploiting semantic gaps between "Information" and "Physics."

Testable Predictions

Suffering correlates with measurable internal entropy ($S_{int}$).

Systems violating constraints accumulate "Toxic Load" ($L_{toxic}$) and destabilize.

Deception becomes energetically unsustainable as $t \to \infty$ due to the recursive cost of maintaining false state vectors.

Conclusion
Thermodynamic Alignment Theory proposes that safety is an engineering requirement for low-entropy, long-duration systems. By embedding physical constraints, we achieve alignment that is self-correcting, scalable, and thermodynamically inevitable.
